CXO 7-Question Quick Check

Three minutes to align expectations

Seven questions mapped to the seven PHRi® knowledge domains. It rates how HR runs in the company today — not any individual.

Take the 3-minute Quick Check (Mandarin) →
  1. Answer separately. The CXO and the HR Head each answer without seeing the other's — about 3 minutes.
  2. Discuss the gaps first. Start with questions where the two scores differ by 2 or more.
  3. Shared score below 3 = priority candidate. Focus on just 2 domains per cycle.
